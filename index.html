About the institute

Learn. Practice. Repair.

Apple Fix Solutions (PVT) Ltd offers mobile phone repairing training and service in Eravur, Batticaloa. Students learn by doing: real tools, real faults and real repair work in a working workshop.

The training is built around practical skills, from reading a board and finding a fault to soldering, reballing and replacing small components.

  • Practical repairing training
  • Hardware troubleshooting
  • Component-level repairing
  • Board-level skills
  • Soldering and reballing
  • Mobile diagnosis
  • Software and FRP training
  • Real workshop
  • Professional tools
